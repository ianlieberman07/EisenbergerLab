# Inspiration image manifest

17 images supplied by the client (Dr. Naomi Eisenberger) to communicate the visual
register she wants. **These are mood references, not necessarily site assets.**

⚠️ **Licensing:** all but one are Shutterstock files. Original filenames are kept
below. Do not publish any of these without confirming the lab holds a license for
that specific asset.

## The through-line

Surreal, conceptual, editorial. Sculptural human heads and busts. Figures
half-submerged in still water. Muted, desaturated palettes — charcoal, slate,
sage, bone, dusty rose. Recurring motif of **two entities in relation**: paired
heads, clasped hands, a brain tethered to a heart. That motif is the lab's
research thesis; use it.

Minority register: bold flat illustration with saturated color. Accent only.
