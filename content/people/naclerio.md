Maria Naclerio received her BA in Psychology from Duke University. After graduating, she spent a year volunteering with AmeriCorps in rural Alaska and two years conducting clinical research on suicidality and self-harm at McLean Hospital/Harvard Medical School. At UCLA, Maria's research focuses on prosocial behavior, social connection, and well-being. She is interested in understanding how helping others can improve mental health, the mechanisms underlying these effects, and how to increase prosociality. Outside of the lab, Maria loves being outdoors on a trail or in the ocean.
