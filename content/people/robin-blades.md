I am interested in the impact of chronic stress on wellness, particularly through inflammatory pathways. In my work, I examine neuro-immune interactions and develop mind-body interventions that promote resilience. My current work focuses on how threat and reward pathways can be leveraged to enhance immune health. I am a health psychology graduate student at the University of California, Los Angeles.
