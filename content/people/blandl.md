Flora Blandl received her B.S. in Psychology with a minor in American Sign Language from The Ohio State University. Flora is interested in the biobehavioral pathways linking social (dis)connection to emotional and physical health. Specifically, her research examines how loneliness and interventions aimed at facilitating social well-being affect the brain, behavior, and immune processes (e.g. inflammation). In her free time, Flora enjoys going to the beach, on hikes, and to get ice cream, all in the company of her dog, Milky.
